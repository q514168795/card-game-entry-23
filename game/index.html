HOW TO PLAY

Reduce your rival alchemist from 30 life to zero. Your own life must hold.

1 · ManaYour crystals refill each turn and grow to 10. Cards cost mana; the number in the top-left gem.
2 · PlayClick a card in your hand, then click an empty slot on your battlefield (or a target for spells).
3 · AttackClick one of your ready creatures, then click a foe creature or the rival alchemist. Each strikes once per turn unless it has Swift.
4 · TurnPress Space or End Turn. New turn = refill mana, draw a card, ready your creatures.

Keywords: Rush ready on entry · Guard forces all attacks onto it first · Ward immune to spells · Bulwark takes 1 less damage · Swift strikes twice · Burn damages the rival on entry.

Keys: 1–9 play card · Space end turn · Esc cancel · Shift+R restart
